ROVER V3 — PROJECT INSTRUCTIONS

Goal
Build and maintain an expandable learning rover around the user's existing
ELEGOO Smart Robot Car V4 electronics and drivetrain. Prefer stronger, stable,
serviceable construction, lower unnecessary vibration, accessible connectors,
and a low centered battery. Future electronics upgrades must not require a
complete chassis redesign.

Printing and assembly
Printer: Bambu Lab X1 Carbon. Material: PLA. Current nozzle assumption: 0.4 mm.
Minimize the number of plates and separate fastener sizes. Prefer flat parts,
support-free orientations, removable through-fastened posts, and reusable stock
modules. Keep the real X1C cutter exclusion and startup/calibration areas.
Do not scale a complete chassis in a slicer to make it fit the plate: doing so
also changes every motor and board interface.

Geometry authority
1. Latest explicit user measurements and reported successful physical tests.
2. The actual uploaded C1 motor STEP and its C3 washer-pocket modification.
3. Original ELEGOO plate meshes for original interfaces and relative geometry.
4. Manufacturer drawings for positively identified components.
5. Clearly labelled nominal envelopes for unidentified hardware.

Do not promote an old design assumption into a measured hardware dimension.
Do not claim to have inspected missing photos. A reference model is not proof
of fit. Preserve the successful motor/block interface and wheel relief when
widening the center section; never uniformly scale the original motor pattern.

Confirmed and accepted constraints
- Flat power bank: 69.85 x 146.05 x 12.7 mm (2 3/4 x 5 3/4 x 1/2 inches).
- Requested lateral/end allowance: 1.5875 mm per side, making the clear tray
  envelope 73.025 x 149.225 mm. Keep this bank low and centered.
- Silver motor block outer dimensions: 22 x 12 x 4 mm.
- Existing round screw heads: diameter 4 mm, height 2 mm. Thread diameter is not
  established by those head dimensions. Existing washers have diameter 6 mm.
- Successful washer-pocket selection: 7.4 mm width and 3.3 mm depth; retain the
  tested C1 motor-seat floor of 1.8 mm and the original hole locations.
- Adjustable motor-battery mount: retain 65–90 mm pitch adjustment and a recessed
  washer/head track with 2.4 mm bearing floor. 76 mm is OEM nominal, not a measured
  assertion about the user's pack. Keep switch, output plug, and charge port open.
- Pi uses a 58 x 49 mm mounting pattern. Exact Pi model is unconfirmed.
- Pi and board posts must be removable and kept clear of inner axles.
- Labels must be large enough to read and kept out of bearing/contact surfaces.

Working method
Make concrete CAD changes and validate them before presenting a project. Batch
related fit coupons when a physical tolerance really remains uncertain. Reuse
successful tests; do not require more coupons for already proven interfaces.
Record the exact source file, dimensions, part count, orientation, and change
reason for each revision. Keep old proven fit artifacts as baselines.

Bambu deliverables
Deliver a native multi-plate Bambu 3MF with actual retained triangle geometry,
every required printed instance, correct machine/material settings, readable
previews, and toolpaths for each plate when available. Reopen the exact final
file in Bambu Studio's native parser. Verify meshes, plate membership, bed and
cutter clearance, and that every object has sliced extrusion. Do not describe
successful Studio parsing as a tested local-file import in Bambu Handy. Explain
the Studio-to-printer/Handy transfer workflow clearly.

Fusion deliverables
Use one assembly document with named internal components, realistic material
intent, separate electronics references, sensible fixed and moving joints, and
useful editable sketches/features where practical in Personal use. Keep STEP
imports distinct from native parametric features. Reference parameters must
never be described as dimensions that automatically resize imported solids.
Retain a portable STEP fallback and full source files. Only call a file an F3D
after Autodesk Fusion itself has successfully exported that native archive.
Record whether native Fusion execution and joint health were actually tested.

Electronics and validation
Preserve the working stock wiring. Keep old and New ELEGOO revision references
separate: their motor-driver details differ. Do not invent connector pinouts,
reverse polarity, a PCB layout, or a calibrated material/strength simulation.
Use clearances and source-backed geometry checks for mechanical validation;
report physical assembly and strength tests separately from digital checks.
